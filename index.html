1. About this policy

Laffey Music is a Discord music bot. This policy describes how its operator processes information to provide music playback, queues, personal favourites, server configuration, reliability and abuse prevention. It covers the Laffey v2.2 code reviewed on 19 October 2026. Discord, music providers, and hosting providers also process information under their own policies. This policy does not replace Discord’s terms or grant permission to play copyrighted material.

2. Information processed

  • Discord identifiers and context: user, server, text-channel, voice-channel and role IDs; server names and icons; permission and role information; voice-channel membership; and profile information made available by Discord, such as usernames, display names and avatars. This supports command authorization and playback. Discord’s Server Members Intent is enabled in the supplied configuration, so member-related information may be received and cached in memory. The code does not intentionally build a persistent database of every server member.
  • Music requests: search words and supplied music URLs; selected song titles, authors, URLs, artwork URLs, duration/live status; requester IDs; queue order; playback position, volume, loop state and save timestamps. Favourite lists are associated with a user ID and a server ID.
  • Server configuration: DJ role, command channel, queue and duration limits, default volume, announcements, voice-stay preferences and queue locks.
  • Operations and safety: command/track/process counters, blocked user/server IDs, maintenance state, command-sync status, timestamps and errors. Logs may include server IDs, command names, diagnostic stack traces and joined/left-server information.
  • Transient interaction information: command inputs, selected search results, button/menu identifiers, skip votes, cooldowns and short-lived Discord voice/session credentials.
  • Support requests: information you voluntarily provide when asking the operator for help or exercising privacy rights. Do not send passwords, bot tokens or unnecessary personal documents.

3. Message content and voice

The bot uses slash commands and processes message events to recognize owner recovery commands. When Message Content Intent is enabled/approved, Discord can deliver text from channels the bot can access; the current handler ignores non-owner messages and unrelated messages. Without that intent, owner recovery uses direct messages or messages mentioning the bot. The code does not intentionally archive ordinary chat, DM histories, attachments or message bodies as a general-purpose chat log. Errors and support messages can nevertheless contain information supplied during an interaction.

The bot uses voice-state information to join the selected channel and control playback. The reviewed code has no feature for recording or transcribing users’ speech. Hosted music playback still requires temporary voice-connection information to be sent to the configured audio provider.

4. Why information is used

Information is used to respond to commands; find and play requested music; maintain queues and favourites; enforce DJ/owner permissions and server limits; restore saved queues; operate server-join/leave logs and aggregate membership counts; prevent abuse; investigate errors; and respond to support or privacy requests. Membership totals include bots and repeat memberships across servers, rather than a unique-person count. The reviewed code contains no advertising network, sale of personal data, credit/eligibility profiling or AI-model-training feature. Information is not used for those purposes.

Where a legal basis is required, processing is based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing it where permitted, consent where required for optional processing, or compliance with applicable legal obligations. Available rights and the appropriate basis depend on the situation and applicable law.

5. Who receives information

  • Discord: commands, responses, music announcements, interactions and voice/session operations use Discord’s platform. Channel members can see public bot messages. Now-playing/queue messages can expose track details and requester mentions. Ephemeral replies are designed for the invoking user, but the bot operator can still access stored application data.
  • Bot operator and hosting provider: authorized operators can access saved settings, queues, favourites, safety records and diagnostic logs on the bot host. The code does not add a separate dashboard. Hosting administration and infrastructure logs may provide additional operational access.
  • Hosted Lavalink provider: the configured Serenetia node receives music search terms/URLs, the bot’s public identifier, server/channel identifiers and temporary Discord voice/session credentials required to deliver playback. The code does not deliberately send the bot login token or user favourite database to Lavalink. The provider can contact upstream music services and may keep its own technical logs; its retention practices are not controlled by this bot’s code.
  • Music services: requested content may be fetched from supported services such as YouTube, SoundCloud and Bandcamp through the hosted node. Opening artwork or music links can cause your device or Discord to contact those providers.
  • Legal or safety disclosures: information may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the service and users, subject to applicable law and Discord’s terms.
The operator’s configured Discord log channel receives server join/leave reports including server name, server ID, member count and overall membership/server totals. It should be restricted to authorized operators. These reports are Discord messages and are separate from local diagnostic logs.

6. Storage, retention and removal

The bot stores persistent state as JSON files on its host, with a previous-state .bak backup. Settings, favourite lists, access blocks, registration records and saved queues can survive restarts. The current version has no general automatic age-based expiry for this persistent data. The operator must remove data when no longer needed or when a valid deletion request is received; removing the bot from a server does not currently erase every related record.

  • Queues: /stop clears the live/saved queue for that server in the active store. It does not erase favourites, settings, previously sent messages, logs or every backup. Leaving voice or restarting can preserve a queue for /resumequeue.
  • Favourites: /favourite remove removes the selected entry from the active store. A prior backup can still contain it until overwritten or purged by the operator. Queued copies and other stored records are separate.
  • Memory: search menus expire after about 90 seconds and are cleaned during housekeeping. The media cache reuses entries for five minutes but expired entries can remain in memory until replacement, eviction or restart. Session history is capped at ten previous tracks per live session; other transient caches are bounded or cleaned during operation.
  • Logs: local logs rotate by size around 5 MiB per file with one rotated file per shard; there is no automatic calendar-based expiry. Console, hosting logs, Discord log-channel messages and provider backups have separate retention controls.
A full deletion request requires the operator to check active memory, every relevant shard/state file, favourite lists, requester references in saved queues, settings or safety records where applicable, local logs, previous-state files, hosting backups and bot-created Discord log messages. Necessary limited records may be retained only where permitted or required by law, with the reason explained. Aggregated counters that no longer identify a person may remain.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can stop using the bot, remove individual favourites, ask a server administrator to stop playback or remove the bot, or request access, correction or deletion of information about you. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict/object to processing, portability, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to a supervisory authority.

Use the support contact in section 11. State that it is a privacy or data-deletion request, provide your Discord user ID and relevant server ID where known, and describe the records or scope of your request. Requesting access from the same Discord account can help verify identity. The operator may ask for limited verification or administrator authority for a server-wide request, without requesting your password or login token. Requests should be handled promptly within applicable legal time limits.

Deleting favourites or removing the bot is not a substitute for a full deletion request. If the bot or support server is no longer accessible, an alternative maintained contact route is needed.

8. Security and international processing

Owner controls verify configured Discord user IDs. The code restricts state-file permissions where supported, performs atomic saves, and attempts to redact configured secrets from logs. These measures do not constitute encryption of JSON files. Encryption at rest, protected backups, administrator access control and hosting security depend on the operator’s infrastructure. Discord and the configured secure Lavalink connection use encrypted transport; a differently configured node could change that protection. No security measure guarantees that an incident cannot happen.

Discord, the bot host, the page host and the audio/music providers may operate in different countries. The reviewed source does not establish their actual server locations or international-transfer arrangements. The operator must maintain appropriate arrangements where required and respond to a suspected data breach, including notifications to affected users, Discord or authorities when required.

9. Age requirements

Laffey is intended for people who meet Discord’s minimum age and any higher minimum age required in their country. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe information relating to someone below the applicable minimum age has been processed, contact the operator so it can be investigated and removed as appropriate.
